Proximal tubule — approximate reabsorption

Percentage of the filtered load reabsorbed along the entire proximal tubule (early + late segments), under normal conditions.

Glucose~100%
Amino acids~100%
Na⁺~65–70%

K⁺~65%

Lactate / citrateMost (>90%)*

*Lactate and citrate handling varies with plasma concentration and acid–base status. Organic anions and cations are predominantly secreted here rather than assigned a single reabsorption percentage. These are teaching approximations, not measurements for this individual early-proximal-tubule cell; drug and disease buttons do not recalculate the reference percentages.
